The gear

Four modules. One colour each.

Orange for the amp, green for the looper, purple for the camera, yellow for export. You always know where you are, even in the middle of a solo.

01

The amp that sounds like the real thing

Load a NAM capture, add a cab impulse response, set three knobs. No plugin to host, no chain to build.

  • Ready-to-play NAM amp captures
  • 3-band EQ and impulse response
  • ~10 ms latency with an audio interface
02

A looper that stays put

Record, loop, stack. The metronome is locked to the transport, not to your mood. One layer too many? Undo.

  • Record → loop → overdub
  • Undo and clear in one gesture
  • Loops are saved with the project
03

Camera and sound line up

Roadie films and records at the same time, then measures the offset between picture and sound. One chord, and everything is realigned.

  • Live preview in a floating monitor
  • Audio + video takes together
  • Built-in sync calibration
04

Editing, GarageBand-style

Move, trim, split, duplicate. Cycle zone, zoom, undo and redo. You edit the take the way you played it: by hand.

  • Editable regions on every track
  • ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z on every gesture
  • Collapsible panels, dark and light themes
